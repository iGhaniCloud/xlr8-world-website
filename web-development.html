How It Works

From Wireframe to Live Site

Our transparent development process keeps you informed and in control at every stage — from initial design concepts through to launch day and beyond.

01

Discovery & Scoping

We gather requirements, define goals, audit your existing site (if applicable), and deliver a detailed project scope, sitemap, and timeline before a single line of code is written.

02

Design & Prototyping

High-fidelity wireframes and interactive prototypes for your review — we iterate on design until every page feels exactly right before we move into development.

03

Development & QA

Clean, semantic, well-documented code built to your approved designs — with cross-browser testing, accessibility checks, performance optimization, and full QA before launch.
